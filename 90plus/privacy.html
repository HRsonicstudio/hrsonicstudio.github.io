90+ – Live Scores: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains what information the 90+ – Live Scores Android app ("90+", "the app") handles. The app is made by HR Sonic Studio ("we"). 90+ doesn't need an account, and we don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Information stored on your phone

Your settings (language, theme, time format, notification choices), the teams, competitions and matches you follow, and your recent searches are saved only on your device. We can't see them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Football data and news

To show scores, line-ups, tables and headlines, the app downloads public football data and images from ysscores.com. Tapping a headline opens the full story on that website in your browser. Like any website you visit, these servers receive your IP address and basic technical information about the request (such as the app version and device type). We don't send them any information that identifies you.

Notifications

Kick-off reminders and goal alerts are created on your phone for the matches and teams you follow. No notification data is sent to us. You can turn notifications off in the app's settings or in your phone's settings at any time.

Advertising

The app may show ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your approximate location, based on your IP address,
  • information about how you interact with the app and its ads,
  • diagnostic information such as crash logs and performance data.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Learn how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

90+ is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices and deleting data

We don't keep any of your data on our own servers, so there's nothing for us to delete. To remove everything the app has stored, clear the app's storage or uninstall it. For data collected by Google AdMob, see Google's policy linked above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
